Layer 03 · Email

Strong policy, one unsigned sender

SPF hard fail, DMARC full reject, clean blacklists. Mailgun is authorised in SPF but has no DKIM signature — under p=reject, a forwarded Mailgun message can fail outright.

  • Free fix: publish Mailgun DKIM — about fifteen minutes in the console.
  • Then: isolate bulk mail on a dedicated sending subdomain.
  • Google and SendGrid already sign; Mailgun is the only gap.
