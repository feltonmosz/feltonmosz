Block 03 · Email

80% bulletproof

Strict SPF, DMARC at reject, clean blacklists. One real gap: HubSpot mail has no DKIM signature, so DMARC passes only via SPF.

  • Free fix: connect the HubSpot sending domain, add the two DKIM records, confirm dkim=pass.
  • Then: move bulk mail to email.budibase.com so one campaign can't hurt the root domain.
  • Check: Mailchimp k1 and SES Easy DKIM in-console.
